About us

Founded in 2012, Decibels & Pixels stands on a strong foundation backed by over 27 years of industry expertise. Headquartered in Chennai, we proudly serve clients across India. Over the years, we’ve evolved into a trusted leader in audio-visual solutions, blending innovation with reliability to deliver impactful experiences. Our journey has been defined by a commitment to excellence, deep client understanding, and the ability to transform complex requirements into seamless, future-ready solutions
